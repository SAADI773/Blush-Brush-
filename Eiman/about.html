Our Heritage & Passion

Crafting Unforgettable Beauty Experiences Since 2018

Blush & Brush was founded with a simple yet ambitious vision: to create a peaceful sanctuary where modern beauty treatments meet world-class hospitality. We believe that true elegance comes from feeling relaxed, comfortable, and cared for.

Over the years, our salon has grown into a trusted destination for hair transformations, rejuvenating skin therapy, and bespoke bridal styling. Every detail in our house—from our serene interior aesthetics to our organic products—is designed around your comfort.
